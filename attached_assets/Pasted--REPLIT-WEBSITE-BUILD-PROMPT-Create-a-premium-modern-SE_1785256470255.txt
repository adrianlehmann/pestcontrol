# REPLIT WEBSITE BUILD PROMPT

Create a premium, modern, SEO-optimized website for **W4 Pest Management**, a trusted pest control company serving Troy, Michigan and the surrounding Metro Detroit area. The website should immediately establish trust, professionalism, and peace of mind while encouraging visitors to request an inspection or call for immediate service.

The design should feel modern, clean, and reassuring—not overly corporate or overly aggressive. The goal is to make homeowners and businesses feel confident that W4 Pest Management will solve their pest problems quickly, safely, and professionally.

The primary goal of the website is to generate phone calls, quote requests, and recurring pest maintenance plan sign-ups.

# Visual Inspiration

The website should project cleanliness, safety, and professionalism while reassuring visitors that pest problems can be solved quickly and effectively. Use high-quality imagery of technicians performing inspections, treated homes, healthy outdoor spaces, and close-up photography of common pests in tasteful, non-alarming ways. Incorporate subtle animations, modern icons, and nature-inspired green accents to reinforce trust and expertise. The overall impression should be that W4 Pest Management is the knowledgeable local expert homeowners can rely on for both immediate pest solutions and long-term protection through recurring maintenance plans.
